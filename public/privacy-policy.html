Privacy Policy

Solitaire (com.paultranvan.solitaire) · Effective date: 12 November 2026

Solitaire is a single-player card game developed by Paul Tran-Van ("we", "us"). It has no accounts, no advertising and no tracking. Nothing leaves your device unless you choose to publish a score to the world leaderboard, and we never sell or share data.

Data stored on your device

To make the game work, the app saves the following locally, in your device's private app storage (or your browser's storage for the web version):

  • Your current game in progress, so you can resume it later.
  • Your game statistics and records (wins, times, scores).
  • Your preferences (such as draw count, language, sound settings, and your leaderboard choice and display name).

We cannot see any of it. Uninstalling the app, or clearing the app's data in your device settings, permanently deletes it. If you export your statistics to a backup file, that file goes only where you save it.

World leaderboard (optional)

The app can publish your winning games to a leaderboard shared by all players. The first time you win a game, the app asks whether you want to take part. Nothing is sent before you answer, and if you choose "Don't take part", nothing is ever sent. You can change your answer at any time with the "World leaderboard" switch in the settings.

What is sent

If you take part, each game you win sends the following to our leaderboard server:

  • The display name you chose. The app suggests a random pseudonym, which you can edit; it is shown publicly, so do not enter anything you would not want others to read.
  • The game itself: the identifier of the deal, the draw mode, the list of moves you played, and how long the game took. The server replays the moves to check that the win is genuine and to compute the score.

If you are offline, the game waits on your device and is sent when the connection returns.

The first time you take part, the app also sends, once, your best wins from before 1 December 2026 (up to three per draw mode) so that they appear on the leaderboard: for each, the identifier of the deal, the draw mode, the duration, the number of moves and the date it was won. The question you answer before anything is sent tells you when this applies.

What is kept and shown

For each published game the server keeps the display name, the score, the duration, the number of moves, the deal identifier, the draw mode, the date, and whether the game could be checked by replaying its moves. The display name, score, duration, number of moves and date are visible to everyone who opens the leaderboard. The list of moves is used for the check and is not kept.

The server receives no account, email address, device identifier, advertising identifier, or location. An entry is not linked to you or to your device in any way other than the display name you gave it.

Viewing the leaderboard

Opening the "World" tab of the records downloads the current leaderboard from the server, whether or not you take part. This request carries no information about you or your games.

Hosting and your IP address

The leaderboard is hosted by Cloudflare, with its database located in Western Europe. As with any internet request, your IP address reaches Cloudflare when the app contacts the server. It is used to deliver the response and to limit abuse (a cap on requests per minute). We do not store it with leaderboard entries and we keep no log of individual requests. Cloudflare handles it under its own privacy policy.

Retention and removal

Leaderboard entries are kept for as long as the leaderboard exists. Turning the "World leaderboard" switch off stops any further sending but does not remove entries already published. To have an entry removed, write to the address below with its display name and approximate date. Because entries are not tied to an identity, we may ask for a detail such as the score to find the right one. We may also remove entries whose display name is offensive or misleading.

Publishing is based on your consent, which you can withdraw at any time as described above. If you are in the European Union you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority.

Network and tracking

Apart from the leaderboard requests described above, the app makes no network requests and works fully offline. It contains no analytics, no advertising, no tracking or fingerprinting technologies, and no third-party SDKs that collect data. It does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, or files.

The web version is served by GitHub Pages; loading it involves GitHub in the same way as visiting any website.

Permissions

The app may use device vibration (haptic feedback) to enhance gameplay, and internet access for the world leaderboard only. Neither involves collecting any data beyond what is described above.

Children's privacy

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The only information that can leave the device is a chosen display name and game results, and only if the leaderboard is used. A parent or guardian can ask us to remove an entry at the address below.
